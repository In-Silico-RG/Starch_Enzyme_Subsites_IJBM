Highlights
- Contact map of glucose oxygens at each subsite of four starch-active enzyme families
- Side chains binding the rings next to the cleaved bond are the same in every group
- Conserved residues hold O2, O3 and O6 at the -1 subsite; O6 is mostly free elsewhere
- Substitution at O6 is expected to obstruct hydrolysis less than at O2 or O3
