Fixed and variable sugar contacts at the subsites of starch-degrading enzymes: a structural basis for processing functionalised starch

Highlights
- Census of 637 PDB entries of 14 starch-active families maps sugar contacts by subsite
- 11 of 13 side-chain hydrogen-bonders at subsites -1/+1 are fixed in every lineage
- Aromatic residues keep an aromatic ring; aliphatic residues change between lineages
- Residues of every kind at the outer subsites vary between lineages
- Hydrogen-bonding positions exceed aliphatic ones in conservation by 0.33 (p = 0.003)
