Fixed and variable sugar contacts at the subsites of starch-degrading enzymes: a structural basis for processing functionalised starch

Highlights
- Subsite × oxygen map of glucose contacts in GH13, GH31, GH14, GH15 (114 PDB entries)
- 11 of 14 side-chain hydrogen-bonders at subsites -1/+1 are fixed in every group
- O2, O3, O6 at -1 and O3 at +1 lie next to invariant residues; O6 is mostly free
- Map matches 12 of 14 testable literature cells, more than trivial or permuted maps
- Hypothesis: substitution at O6 obstructs hydrolysis less than at O2 or O3
