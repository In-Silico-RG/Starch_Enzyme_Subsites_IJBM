\noindent\textbf{A fixed core and a variable rim: hydroxyl contacts at the subsites of $\alpha$-amylases and related starch hydrolases, compared with the hydrolysis of substituted starch}

\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Subsite $\times$ oxygen map of glucose contacts in GH13, GH31, GH14, GH15 (114 PDB entries)
  \item 11 of 14 side-chain hydrogen-bonders at subsites $-1$/$+1$ are fixed in every group
  \item Conserved polar residues hold O2, O3, O6 at $-1$ in GH13, GH31 and GH15; O6 mostly free
  \item 12 of 14 `not tolerated' cells agree; a subsite-only rule gets 10 of 12
  \item Hypothesis: substitution at O6 obstructs hydrolysis less than at O2 or O3
\end{itemize}
