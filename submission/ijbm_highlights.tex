\noindent\textbf{A structural map of glucose-hydroxyl recognition in $\alpha$-amylases and $\alpha$-glucosidases and its implications for the enzymatic hydrolysis of chemically modified starch}

\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Contact map of glucose oxygens at each subsite of four starch-active enzyme families
  \item Side chains binding the rings next to the cleaved bond are the same in every group
  \item Conserved residues hold O2, O3 and O6 at the $-1$ subsite; O6 is mostly free elsewhere
  \item Substitution at O6 is expected to obstruct hydrolysis less than at O2 or O3
\end{itemize}
